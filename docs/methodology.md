# Methodology

## 1. Framework
| Step | Approach |
|---|---|
| Scope | Tata Motors Ltd (TMCV), consolidated, India CV business. Iveco (acquired Oct-2026) excluded and treated as value-neutral. |
| Valuation date | 08-Nov-2026 NSE close (TMCV and all peers priced from the same close). |
| Primary method | Trading comparables on FY28E (12-month-forward year): EV/EBITDA and P/E, 50/50. |
| Cross-check | FCFF DCF, Gordon-growth terminal value; flagged if more than ±20% from the target. |
| Rating bands | BUY > +15%, HOLD −5% to +15%, SELL < −5% expected 12-month return. |

## 2. Forecast drivers (Base case)
| Driver | FY27E | FY28E | FY29–31E | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue growth | 16% | 9% | 8% / 8% / 7% | H1 FY27 +28%; H2 guided high-single to low-double digit; ICRA 4–6%, CRISIL 5–6% industry growth |
| EBITDA margin | 10.8% | 11.5% | 12.0% | ~540–590 bp commodity hit in H1, further 150–200 bp guided for Q3; price hikes catch up |
| Capex (% revenue) | 3.0% | 3.0% | 3.0% | Guided 2–4% |
| D&A (% revenue) | 2.3% | 2.3% | 2.3% | FY26 level |
| Working capital (days) | R 12 / I 24 / P 66 | same | same | FY26 levels; part of the Q2 FY27 release reverses |
| Tax rate | 26% | 26% | 26% | 25.17% statutory (s.115BAA) + tax on dividends from subsidiaries |
| Payout | 30% | 30% | 30% | FY26 DPS ₹4 |

Bull / bear cases flex growth (19% / 12% FY27E; 12% / 2% FY28E), margin (up to 13% / down to 9.5%) and capex (2.75% / 3.5%).

## 3. Trading comparables
- **Peers:** Ashok Leyland (standalone), Mahindra & Mahindra (standalone), SML Mahindra. Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai and Eicher were screened out — passenger vehicles, or a CV business (VECV) that is equity-accounted inside a two-wheeler-led group.
- **Standalone basis** for Ashok Leyland and M&M, because consolidated borrowings include Hinduja Leyland Finance and Mahindra Finance.
- **Net debt** = borrowings incl. leases − (cash + bank balances + current investments); strategic stakes excluded; treasury investments included where they are financial assets.
- **Forward estimates:** broker averages (BOBCAPS, ICICI Direct for Ashok Leyland; MOFSL, PL, BOBCAPS for M&M); SML Mahindra has no coverage, so its estimates are the author's own.
- **Target multiple:** peer median less 10% — M&M's market cap includes listed stakes that its standalone EBITDA excludes (~15–18% inflation), and TMCV is more exposed to the cyclical heavy-truck segment. Result: 12.7x EV/EBITDA, inside the 12–14x broker range.

## 4. DCF cross-check
- **Cost of equity** = 7.12% (10Y G-Sec) + 1.20 × 5.2% = 13.4%. ERP is Damodaran's India total ERP (7.08%) less the 1.87% sovereign default spread already priced into the INR risk-free rate. Beta is a cyclical-CV proxy because TMCV has about a year of trading history.
- **WACC** 12.6% (10% target debt weight; pre-tax Kd 8.0%). **Terminal growth** 5.0%. Terminal value = Gordon growth only; implied exit multiple ≈ 8x EV/EBITDA.
- Stub-period and mid-year discounting; equity bridge uses FY26 cash rolled forward to the valuation date (FCFF earned to date less the FY26 dividend) plus investments at book, less borrowings.
- 12-month value = value today × (1 + CoE) − next-year DPS, for comparability with the 12-month target.

## 5. Adjusted earnings
- FY26: reported PAT ₹3,030 Cr; adjusted ₹6,410 Cr after adding back ₹1,428 Cr exceptional items and the ₹2,418 Cr fair-value loss on investments, tax-effected at 25.17%.
- LTM (Dec-25 to Sep-26): ₹7,040 Cr adjusted vs ₹6,308 Cr reported — rebuilt from operating lines (EBITDA − D&A − interest + recurring other income) and cross-checked against reported pre-exceptional PBT.

## 6. Reverse DCF
Holding WACC, terminal growth and the forecast path constant, the CMP of ₹426 requires a uniform margin uplift equivalent to a **~15.2% steady-state EBITDA margin**, or **~7.3% terminal growth**, or a **~10.6% WACC**.

## 7. Known limitations
- Only three listed peers; SML Mahindra estimates are the author's own.
- FY22–FY25 history is segment-level (P&L only); FY22 and FY25 EBITDA derived from reported margins (±0.05% of revenue).
- Iveco, the Tata Capital stake (~₹15–18 / share) and any acquisition financing are outside the estimates.
- Terminal value is ~74% of DCF enterprise value — typical for a five-year explicit forecast.
